fixed        -> 0 DOF

revolute     -> 1 rotational DOF (limited)

continuous   -> 1 rotational DOF (unlimited)

prismatic    -> 1 translational DOF

screw        -> 1 DOF
                translation coupled to rotation

cylindrical  -> 2 DOF
                rotation + translation along same axis

planar       -> 3 DOF
                x, y, yaw

ball         -> 3 rotational DOF

universal    -> 2 rotational DOF
                two perpendicular axes
